Logo review

Your logo is right. Three small things are not.

Prepared for Leslie Roulias · Site build · July 2026

I am not proposing a new logo. The poppy and the Futura lettering are working, and the mark is the strongest asset you have. What follows is three small corrections, all of them adjustments to the artwork you already own.

All of these are adjustments to the existing artwork, not redesigns.

01

The word FINANCIAL is spaced too far apart

The letters are spread so wide that the eye reads nine separate letters instead of one word. The gaps between the letters are actually wider than the letters themselves. It shows up worst in the site's top bar, where the logo is small and that line nearly disappears.

Current
Reads as separate letters
Proposed
Reads as one word

Recommended. Both panels are your real Futura letters at full size. The tighter version is the same nine letters, individually re-spaced, so nothing is redrawn and no new font is needed. The second line also drops very slightly, which the tighter word needs so the tail of the y in "My" stays clear of it. It is the single biggest improvement available for the least work.

02

The poppy needs a simpler version for small sizes

The poppy is drawn with a stem, leaves, and fine roots. Below about the size of a thumbnail those lines are thinner than a single pixel and the flower stops looking like a flower. This is the same problem as the browser tab icon, and it also affects social media avatars and anywhere the mark appears small.

Current mark, made small
Roots and stem turn to haze
What is needed

A second, simplified poppy: fewer petals, no stem, no roots. Drawn once, used only at small sizes.

Holds its shape anywhere

Recommended, and it needs your logo designer. A small version is a different drawing, not the same drawing shrunk, so this is not something I can fake convincingly from the picture files I have. It is roughly a ten minute job for whoever drew the original, if they still have the working file.

03

The two lines do not start on the same edge

FINANCIAL starts very slightly to the right of the M above it, because the word was stretched to match the width of "My Camino" rather than lined up on the left. It is five pixels out of five hundred, about one percent.

Your logo, unmodified

Worth fixing, not worth paying for. You would notice it on a banner or a printed sign. You would never notice it on a screen. Have it corrected the next time the logo file is open for another reason, such as the small version above.
