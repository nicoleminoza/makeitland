What you're funding

Citizen Medic Foundation teaches first aid, CPR, and disaster preparedness to people outside medicine, the ones most likely to be first at the scene.

Each workshop trains 30 to 50 people. Students pay $20 to $30, and we waive it for anyone who asks. The Foundation covers the rest, about $1,000 a workshop: extra instructors so the groups stay small enough that everyone practices, mannequin rental, food, and the bandages and slings that get used up. A Supporting Sponsorship covers two of them. A Presenting Sponsorship covers five.

How sponsorships are sold

Sponsorships are sold through a conversation and an invoice, not a checkout. Tell us which tier fits and we'll send terms.

The Presenting Sponsorship closes TBD a date, or when it is taken, whichever comes first. Supporting and Community close when the programs go to print.
