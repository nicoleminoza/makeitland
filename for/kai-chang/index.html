Where the money goes

Citizen Medic Foundation teaches first aid, CPR, and disaster preparedness to people outside medicine, the ones most likely to be first at the scene.

Each workshop trains 30 to 50 people. Students pay $20 to $30, and we waive it for anyone who asks. The Foundation covers the rest, about $1,000 a workshop: extra instructors so the groups stay small enough that everyone practices, mannequin rental, food, and the bandages and slings that get used up.

Before you buy

Is this the same as the public opening?

No. This is a private screening on December 17, before opening weekend. The auditorium is booked for guests of Citizen Medic.

Where do I go, and where do I park?

Everything happens at the Culver Theater. Park in any of the three Culver City lots: 9099 Washington, 3846 Cardiff, or 3844 Watseka. The city covers the first hour, and the theater validates two more, so bring your ticket inside. Three EV charging stations are at 9099 Washington.

What should I wear?

No rule, but past guests have dressed up. Blazers, a good jacket, something with personality. Nobody has ever been overdressed here.

Can I bring my kids?

This one’s for the grown-ups. Drinks are served all night, so every guest needs to be 21 or over.

What if I can’t make it after all?

Your seat is transferable. Email events@citizenmedic.org and we’ll move it to whoever takes your place.

Anything else, including dietary needs, accessibility, and seating a group together, write to events@citizenmedic.org.

Can't make it? Cover the seat you can't use. A gift of any amount is appreciated and fully tax-deductible. We'll send a receipt for your records.
