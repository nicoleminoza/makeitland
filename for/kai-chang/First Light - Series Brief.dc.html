A recommendation for Kai Chang

Give the premiere a name that outlives the film

You have run this event twice and both sold out. Right now each one is a separate thing that happens to have the same host. Naming the series turns them into an annual event people look forward to.

The recommendation

First Light 2026

Dune: Part Three

First Light is the series. The year and the film are the subtitle, and they change every cycle. In 2028 it is First Light 2028 and whatever the film turns out to be, and nothing about the brand has to be rebuilt.

What the guest slot might hold next

2028 looks like Top Gun 3, which would close a loop with the 2022 night. There may also be a 2027 option: Avengers: Secret Wars is expected December 17, 2027, and The Lord of the Rings: The Hunt for Gollum is expected around December 2027.

Three different films, three different worlds, and the series survives all of them unchanged. That is the whole argument for naming it.

The name means the earliest showing. It also carries a first-responder note without ever having to say so, which is the only kind of double meaning worth having.

What stays the same every year

A gala has to invent a theme every year. You get one handed to you by the film, so the parts you fix should be ritual, not decoration.

A private screening, before opening weekend

The auditorium is yours alone. This replaces "first in the world," which the December 14 IMAX preview already breaks this year and which a studio can take away from you any cycle. Private is true forever.

One live auction, one lot worth talking about

Maverick's jacket and helmet in 2022. A hand-forged blade in 2024. Whatever 2026 brings. The making changes every year, the auction does not, and naming the slot means people start guessing in October what the lot will be.

The same shape of evening

Reception, raffle, entertainment, and screening. Returning guests already know how the night works, which is most of what makes someone say yes a second time.

The house rule

Introduce yourself to one person you do not know. It is already on the page and it is the one thing no other premiere does.

One typeface for the series, one guest slot for the film

First Light is set in Cinzel every year and never changes. Below it sits a labelled film slot that borrows the year's film face, Dune Rise for 2026, kept smaller than the series mark and never fused into one lockup with it. The annual change becomes something people watch for instead of a brand you rebuild. The film face stays out of nav, buttons, prices, body copy, and anything sold as an object.

Sponsor tiers that can renew

Presenting Sponsor, one per year and exclusive. Supporting Sponsor, up to four. Film-themed tier names read as fan material in an internal approval request, and they cannot renew, because in 2028 the names change and nobody can buy the same level twice. These two rank themselves without explanation and outlive every film. Keep the film flavor for signage inside the theater.

The part that raises more money

Established benefit series run on co-chairs. Two or three people put their names on the invitation and then sell to their own networks, which is a completely different act from you emailing strangers.

You are currently the only named person on the page, so you are the only one spending reputation on it. Co-chairs for First Light 2026 would give you named hosts, a reason for their networks to buy, and the most natural source of the first sponsors.

It is also the only mechanism that lets this grow past the number of people you personally know.

My recommendations, if you would rather not decide

Keep tickets at $150 and $200. VIP is already the popular tier, which means the ladder works; raising it narrows nothing and risks pushing regulars down. If you want more revenue per head, add a patron seat well above $200 rather than moving the middle.

Publish the theater address and send the after-party address to ticket holders only. It reads as considered rather than secretive, and it gives the confirmation email a reason to be opened.

Non-refundable, transferable. Standard for a benefit, and it lets someone pass a seat to a friend instead of asking you for money back.

Multiple auction lots rather than the forged piece alone. At most benefits the auction out-earns the tickets, and it is currently one line in the schedule.

Yes to seating two Standard seats together, and worth saying so on the page. Nobody comes to a four-hour evening alone.
