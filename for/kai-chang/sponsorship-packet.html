The room

We are assembling this room carefully and with intent

The guest list is built, not sold to whoever shows up. Business owners, operators, and creative talent, roughly 150 of them, in an auditorium booked for our guests alone. Sponsoring puts your company in that room, and in front of it.

The evening is designed so people actually meet. It starts an hour before the film with a reception in the theater itself, a magician working the room table to table and a live auction called from the floor, so the room is already talking by the time the lights go down.

What you're funding

The premiere is how we get people in the room

Citizen Medic Foundation teaches first aid, CPR, and disaster preparedness to people outside the medical field. The skills that matter most in the moment nobody plans for.

Every ticket and every sponsorship goes directly toward training more people to be ready when it counts. What happens after the night is the actual mission.
