First Light

Brand guidelines · Citizen Medic Foundation

An annual benefit screening series. This document is written for whoever builds the next one.

Everything here follows from one decision. First Light is the permanent series. The film is a guest that changes every cycle. The two layers never merge, so the equity built this year survives the next film.

1 · The two-layer system

Layer What it holds
Series
Permanent		 The name First Light, Cinzel, gold, the horizon device, the palette, the tier names, the voice. None of this changes between years.
Film
One year only		 The film title, its typeface, and the hero photograph. Replaced entirely each cycle. It occupies one slot and never spreads beyond it, though within that slot it is the largest thing on the surface.

Text values. #F4EEE4 headings · #EFE7DB body · #DED2BF section headings · #C4B9A6 secondary · #B6AA98 tertiary · #AEA294 labels and eyebrows · #948A7E fine print. Nothing lighter than #948A7E, and never below 12px.

Structural values. Lines, borders, and hairlines only, never as a background or as text. #332C24 section rules · #39312A card and panel borders · #4E4438 the sticky bar edge · #6C5730 secondary button borders and the gold hairline · #7A6234 the pull-quote rule. #E3C179 is the lit state of gold, used on hover and on secondary buttons where filled gold would compete with the primary one.

That is the whole palette: three backgrounds, two golds, ember, seven text steps, five structural values. A value not on this page does not go on a page. Near-misses are the failure mode to watch for, since a bone at #EFE7DA next to one at #EFE7DB reads as carelessness rather than as a second color.

Gold is the action color. If a heading, a label, and a button are all gold, the button stops reading as a button. Section headings are bone, not gold.

3 · Typography

Four faces, each with one job. The rule that keeps it clean: nothing under 13px is ever set in Cinzel.

First Light

Cinzel · display

The wordmark, section headings, card titles, prices, timeline times, primary buttons. Uppercase, tracked .10em to .16em. Never below 13px.

You'll watch it with 150 people who care as much as you do.

EB Garamond · body

All running prose. 17 to 19px on screen, leading 1.55 to 1.7, measure capped at 60 characters.

A Citizen Medic benefit

Archivo · labels

Everything under 13px. Eyebrows, countdown units, tier flags, badges, legal fine print, small buttons. Light 300 for fine print, Regular 400 for labels, Medium 500 for emphasis.

The best night of the year, and I don't even like sci-fi.

Cormorant Garamond Italic · pull quotes

One per page at most. Attributed to a named attendee wherever possible.

4 · The film slot

Each year's film brings its own display face. It gets one slot, larger than the series wordmark, and nothing else. For 2026 the face is Dune Rise by Jesta Designs, under the SIL Open Font License.

Dune

Part Three

Rule
Allowed The film title in the hero slot, the invitation, the ticket, the packet's spec panel, and social art. Uppercase, one size per surface. In a hero it is the largest element in the frame and never under 62px.
Never The words First Light. Navigation, buttons, prices, body copy, the Citizen Medic lockup, or anything sold as an object.
Always A clear size step between the series mark and the film face, so the two never fuse into one lockup. The film face dominates. It is the reason anyone opens the page, and the series earns its permanence from the eyebrow, the palette, and the type rather than from being the biggest word. In the hero the two sit at opposite ends of the image band, series mark small at the top and film face large at the bottom, and that separation replaces the labelled divider. Anywhere the two appear closer together, the label returns. The non-affiliation line stays in the footer. The font credit stays in the fine print, and OFL.txt travels with the font files.

5 · The horizon

The series device. A photographic band, a hairline rule, then the page. It carries the name without illustrating it, and it works with any image in any year.

Band height clamps between 420 and 660px. The composition has two anchors, not one. The series wordmark and the benefit line are pinned to the top of the sky, where they read as the host of the evening. The film face is anchored at the bottom, over the dark mass below the horizon, where it is the largest thing in the frame. The empty sky between them is the whole point of the device, so nothing goes there. The rule at the horizon is a dark hairline, not a gold bar, so the photograph supplies the only warm event. Below it, a soft ochre bleed of about 150px ties the image to the page.

Choose images with an empty upper two-thirds, an unbroken horizon, and no figures. A single small figure in a wide landscape is the film campaign's composition, and using it here turns a structural choice into a resemblance.

6 · Layout

  • One column at 680px, centered, on every page and in every section including the hero. Horizontal padding clamps from 26px to 52px, applied on the outer wrapper so every left edge lines up.
  • Sections are divided by a single 1px #332C24 rule and nothing else. No alternating backgrounds.
  • Groups of siblings use flex or grid with gap. Interactive targets are never below 44px, and buttons never stretch to the full column.

7 · Voice

Plain, specific, unhurried. The night is genuinely good, so the copy does not need to sell it — it needs to describe it accurately.

  • Concrete over abstract. Numbers where they exist. Specifics like the magician, the costumed hostesses, and no phones in the dark do more than any adjective.
  • No exclamation marks, no urgency language, no emoji. Scarcity is stated as fact — the seat count and the sell-out history — never as pressure.
  • Second person for guests, first person plural for the foundation. Every page ends on a person, not a disclaimer.

8 · Fixed wordings

These facts appear on several surfaces and drifted when written twice. Use them verbatim.

Sell-out record The third since 2022, and the first two sold out. Never “sold out every time” in a sentence that includes the coming year — it claims a result the event has not produced yet.
Capacity 150 seats. Stated flat, with no hedge. A precise remaining count beside a hedged total reads as though nobody checked.
Dates Spelled out in prose — Wednesday, December 16. Abbreviations only in the sticky bar, page titles, and meta tags.
The night A private screening, or the premiere. Not a gala, not a private house.

9 · Sponsor tiers

Presenting Sponsor, one per year, exclusive. Supporting Sponsor, up to four. These names outrank each other without explanation, survive any film, and let a sponsor renew at the same level year over year — which film-themed names cannot.

Film-themed naming belongs on signage inside the theater, where it costs nothing and expires harmlessly.
