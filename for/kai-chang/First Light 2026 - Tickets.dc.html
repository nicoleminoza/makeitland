The order of the night

The night starts before the movie does. Everything happens at the Culver Theater, so you arrive once.

6:00

Doors and reception

Food, a bar, and an hour in the room before anyone sits down. A magician works that hour, and you will not always be sure what you just watched. Costumed hostesses appear and recede all evening, leaving small unexplained moments in their wake.

6:45

The auction

Called live from the floor, and what it raises pays for the training.

7:15

Into the auditorium

The whole room moves in together and finds its seats.

7:30

Dune: Part Three

Lights down, before opening weekend, with nobody in the room who wandered in.

After

The after-party

The credits are not the end of it. A smaller room carries the night on for whoever wants to keep talking, close by, with details sent to ticket holders.

You'll also be asked to introduce yourself to at least one person you don't know. That isn't an icebreaker gimmick, it's the house rule.

The circle of people you meet stops growing as you get older unless something forces it back open. For one night, that's the job.

Where the money goes

Citizen Medic Foundation teaches first aid, CPR, and disaster preparedness to people outside the medical field, the skills that matter most in the moment nobody plans for.

Every ticket goes directly toward training more people to be ready when it counts. The premiere is how we get people in the room. What happens after is the actual mission.
