Case study

Translated, not reinvented.

A full practice and a site that said none of it. Positioning, copy, and a new site for My Camino Financial, a money mentor.

See the live site at mycaminofinancial.org

Leslie Roulias, Founder, My Camino Financial

The overlooked thing

Leslie was good at the work. The site was the only place that did not say so.

Fifteen years managing billions across global markets, then she left that for the work that actually called her. Clients describe the result in their own words.

“I gained comprehensive knowledge and clarity about my finances.”

Dinha

“Leslie listened with intention and, in just a few sessions, I have more confidence about how to control my financial future.”

Taryn

The starting point

Her site carried none of it. It spoke in the language of the mission statement, a personal journey, core values, financial wellness, and left a reader to work out who it was for, what happens on a call, and how to start. That mission was the raw material. It was not yet doing the work of a homepage.

Nothing about Leslie changed. Everything about how she shows up did.

The move

The position was already hers. In our first working session, she chose “money mentor” over coach or guide, and that one word decided the rest of the site. It became the headline: your money mentor, wherever you’re starting from.

From there: three named audiences instead of one general one, a three-step path from a free call to an ongoing plan, and one action repeated at the end of every page instead of once in the navigation. Book the call.

Positioning and the money mentor headline
Four pages rewritten and restructured
Identity applied throughout
LinkedIn and Instagram profile and banner assets
What it is

Once the site says what it is, it can say who it is for.

Who it is for

Naming the reader is only half of it. She also has to know what happens next.

The result

Roughly eight of every ten people who booked her free intro call became clients, by Leslie’s own count. The practice was never the problem. The call was already doing the work.

So the site was not built to fix a funnel. It was built to put more of the right people in front of that call.

Conversion tracking goes in with the build, so the next version of this page carries real numbers.

How it worked

One deliverable at a time, staged for review

One round of revisions built into every stage
Thirty days of tweaks after launch
